Rider rules
  • Minimum ages by ride, listed above. Riders under 18 need a parent or guardian's signature.
  • Weight limit [240 lbs] for the comfort and safety of the horses. Close to it? Call us.
  • Closed-toe shoes required. Long pants strongly recommended. Helmets recommended; bring your own.
  • No riding while pregnant or under the influence.
Booking & weather
  • A [50%] deposit holds your spot. Balance due on arrival.
  • Free cancellation up to [72 hours] before. Inside that window, the deposit is forfeited.
  • Rain plan. You have the option to ride in our indoor arena. If lightning, storms, ice, or dangerous heat make riding unsafe, we offer a free reschedule.
  • Arrive 20 minutes early. Late arrivals may shorten your ride.
