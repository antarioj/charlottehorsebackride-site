How the hour goes

Every ride runs on the same plan, so there are no surprises for a nervous rider (or parent).

  1. 0:00

    Meet your horse

    Your guide walks you through the barn and introduces your horse by name.

  2. 0:10

    Ground lesson

    How to sit, hold the reins, steer, and stop. Kids and adults learn side by side.

  3. 0:25

    Mount up

    Mounting block or ramp, guide at your side. Two laps of the paddock until everyone's settled.

  4. 0:35

    On the trail

    Shaded trail, walking pace, single file with the guide up front and a wrangler at the back.

  5. 1:15

    Back at the barn

    Dismount, pet your horse, photos. Ask your guide about next time.

Pack this the night before
  • Long pantsJeans are perfect.
  • Closed-toe shoesSneakers or boots. No sandals.
  • Your own helmetRecommended, but not required. We do not provide one.
  • Water & sunscreenBug spray May–September.
  • Signed waiverWe email it when we confirm.
  • A cameraGuides stop at the best spots.

Rain? We have an indoor arena and we can still ride in the rain. So it does not kill the moment.

Why first-timers do well here

We don't run a trail string that walks nose-to-tail while you hang on. Every rider gets a horse chosen for them, a real lesson, and a guide who's watching. That's the whole idea, and it's why [most] of our riders have never been on a horse before.

  • Calm horses only. Chosen for temperament, trained for trail work with new riders, rested between rides.
  • Small groups. One guide per [8] riders, with a second wrangler on the trail.
  • Plain rules. Ages, weight limit, weather, and cancellations are on the rides page, not in the fine print.
